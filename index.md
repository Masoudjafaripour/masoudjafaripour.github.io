<h2 id="bio">Bio</h2>

<p class="bio-text">
I am an M.Sc. student in the Department of Computing Science at the <a href="https://www.ualberta.ca/">University of Alberta</a>, supervised by <a href="https://webdocs.cs.ualberta.ca/~zaiane/">Prof. Osmar Zaiane</a> and collaborating with <a href="https://drmatttaylor.net/">Prof. Matt Taylor</a>. I am also a Research Scientist Intern at <a href="https://www.ea.com/">Electronic Arts (EA)</a>, training small language models for long-horizon, multi-turn agentic tasks in real-time gameplay. My thesis is focused on empowering multimodal LLM agents with <b>spatial understanding</b> and <b>action prediction</b> by extending inference and context, in particular through test-time scaling and memory-augmented retrieval and token compression, respectively. In addition, I've worked a bit on studying and reviewing dataset-building and curation approaches for fine-tuning VLMs for spatial reasoning, and also a bit on feedback-based in-context learning for LLM agents.
</p>

<details class="more-about-me">
  <summary>More about me</summary>
  <p>Before moving to Computing Science, I was a Ph.D. student in Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering at the University of Alberta, where I worked on robotics. I have 3+ years of academic and industry experience building and deploying deep learning systems. I have also worked on multimodal retrieval (visual RAG, geometric retrieval), visual-tabular representation learning, 3D CAD generation, uncertainty quantification in LLMs, and deep RL for continuous control.</p>
</details>
