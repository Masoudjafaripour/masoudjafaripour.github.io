<h2 id="bio">Bio</h2>

<p class="bio-text">
I am an M.Sc. student researcher in the Department of Computing Science at the <a href="https://www.ualberta.ca/">University of Alberta</a>, supervised by <a href="https://webdocs.cs.ualberta.ca/~zaiane/">Prof. Osmar Zaiane</a> and collaborating with <a href="https://drmatttaylor.net/">Prof. Matt Taylor</a>. I am also a Research Intern at <a href="https://www.ea.com/">Electronic Arts (EA)</a>, working on efficient language models for real-time reasoning and decision-making in games. My thesis studies <b>visual and spatial reasoning in multimodal language models</b>. I work on <b>adaptive, efficient 3D spatial reasoning and action prediction</b> for multimodal agents, including inference-time scaling and context compression, on <b>curating vision-language datasets</b> for embodied agents, and on <b>improving multimodal reasoning</b> through in-context learning, SFT, and RL fine-tuning.
</p>

<details class="more-about-me">
  <summary>More about me</summary>
  <p>Before moving to Computing Science, I was a Ph.D. student in Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering at the University of Alberta, where I worked on robotics. I have 3+ years of academic and industry experience building and deploying deep learning systems. I have also worked on multimodal retrieval (visual RAG, geometric retrieval), visual-tabular representation learning, 3D CAD generation, uncertainty quantification in LLMs, and deep RL for continuous control.</p>
</details>
